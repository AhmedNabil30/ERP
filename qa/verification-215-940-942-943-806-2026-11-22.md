**F-215-1 (rule 8, minor).** On `/projects/new` two rows are active, المشاريع and مشروع جديد, because
`routerLinkActive` uses prefix matching and `/projects` is a prefix of `/projects/new`. AC-215-F's own
wording ("that row, and only that row") would fail if stated for this route. The `/projects` and
`/projects/new` rows were added after this story (KAFF-401, `d72308b`), so this is a regression of rule 8
rather than a gap in the original build. Not fixed here.

**Recommended trailer: `VERIFIED verdict=CONDITIONAL`** — conditions: F-215-1, and AC-215-F / G have no
automated test (proved live only).
