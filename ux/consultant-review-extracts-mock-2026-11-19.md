# Consultant review — extract chain mock (`extracts.html`), 2026-11-19

**Mode:** mock. **Scope:** `.design/v2/team-a/extracts.html`, screens E1–E13 and `#wh`, judged against the screen briefs in `ux/journey-extract-chain-2026-11-18.md` §8 and the rulings D-235, D-238, D-239, D-240. **Roles walked:** site engineer, supervisor, Technical Office, Finance, Owner. **Shots:** 15, at 390 and 1440, light (scratchpad, not kept).

**Re-check.** No earlier consultant review covers this mock, so there is nothing to close. This is the first pass.

**What works.** Every screen does one job and has one primary action in the shell slot. No primary is disabled. No id, enum name or account name shows. «الحجز» and «حركة» follow D-240: «المحجوز» and «قيد» appear nowhere. Extract 3's document reproduces the spec §15 row exactly (400,000 − 80,000 − 100,000 − 30,000 = 190,000). The E5 first payment adds up (188,100 + 1,900 = 190,000 at 1%). The E12 total adds up (78,000 + 7,000 = 85,000).

Findings are ordered by what each one costs the business. **Must** means the mock would teach the build something wrong about money or leave a role stuck. **Should** means a stranger would have to ask. **Could** is noise.

## Codes and database noise

None that should go. The extract number, change-order number, cheque number and transfer reference are kept on purpose and are read aloud by people. «TR-55821» is a bank reference that Finance types, which is fine.

## The three changes I would make first

1. **Make the money agree across E1, E3, E5, E6 and E7 (M1–M4).** Use one cheque, one withheld rate and one "in cheques" figure, put no cheque on an unissued extract, and use material inputs that produce §15. A mock whose figures disagree will be built with figures that disagree, and Finance will stop trusting the screen the first day it differs from the bank.
2. **Rule the dispute and rejection paths before the build (M6, M7).** A quantity dispute must not fall through to a credit note. A rejected extract must land on someone who can change something. These are flow problems, and after the build they cost a lane each.
3. **Fix the words that tell a person who acts next (S10–S14).** That means one form of waiting line, change-order pills in the «عند …» form, and one name each for reversal and payment. These lines are what every role reads first, and today they are inconsistent across screens.
