### R3 Sales

10. Who presses «ضخ من المالك» on the expense screen, and what follows? Not drawn.

### R5 Portal

14. Is «متعثرة» past the planned end and under 100%? Default: yes (derived, needs confirming).
15. Date format: day/month/year or ISO? Default drawn: ISO.
16. Furnishing project: does the Owner choose the contract type? Default: yes.
